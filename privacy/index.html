Privacy policy

This site is a set of static pages. There is no login, no account, and no form that collects personal information.

What is stored in your browser

The only thing this site stores locally is your recently-viewed list, kept in your browser's localStorage so the home page can show it. It never leaves your device, and clearing site data removes it.

Analytics

If analytics is enabled on this deployment, it is used only to count page views and understand which tables are useful. It is not used to build a profile of you or to sell data.

Advertising

This site may display advertising to cover hosting and data costs. Ad networks can set their own cookies and may use them to personalise ads; that behaviour is controlled by the network and by your browser settings, not by this site.

Third-party content

Table data is sourced from community wikis and links out to them. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you follow a link.

Contact

For corrections or questions about this policy, open an issue on the site's public repository.

All games are embedded from their original publishers and remain the property of their authors.
