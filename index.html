How to read a table

  1. Sort by the column that matches your question — $/day for passive income, grow time for fast turnaround.
  2. Search instead of scrolling; 198 rows is a lot of scrolling.
  3. Check the fetch date under the title. An old table is a different table.

Where the numbers come from

Published stats come from community-maintained wikis and are credited on each page. Anything calculated — like $/day — is labelled as calculated. Nothing here is estimated or invented; if a source value is missing, the table shows a dash.

All games are embedded from their original publishers and remain the property of their authors.
