Frequently asked questions

Where do these numbers come from?

Rarity, spawn chance and price are read from the community-maintained Grow a Garden 2 wiki (CC-BY-SA) and credited on this page.

Why is there no income column?

This wiki does not publish an income rate per pet, so adding one would mean guessing. The table only shows what the source actually states.

What does spawn chance mean?

The published probability of the pet appearing. A 2.38% pet will show up far more often than a 0.05% one.
