Frequently asked questions

How is the total calculated?

Income per second of each selected pet, added up. Daily and monthly figures multiply that by 86,400 and 2,592,000.

Does this account for pet limits or slots?

No. It adds up whatever you select. If the game only lets you equip a few at a time, select only those.

Is my selection saved anywhere?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and nothing is stored or sent.
