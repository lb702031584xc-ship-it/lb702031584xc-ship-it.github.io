Frequently asked questions

Where do these numbers come from?

Rarity, income per second, grow time and harvest reward are read from the community-maintained Steal an Egg wiki (CC-BY-SA). The $/day column is calculated by this site.

Why is there no trade value or tier list?

Trade values are player opinions that move several times a day and cannot be verified. Income and grow time are published stats.

How often does the table update?

It is regenerated whenever the wiki data is re-fetched, so edits usually show up within a day.
