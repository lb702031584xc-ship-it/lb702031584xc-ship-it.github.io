Frequently asked questions

What counts as a fast pet?

Anything that pays out in minutes rather than hours. The table is sorted by grow time ascending, so the quickest ones are at the top.

Does a short grow time mean a better pet?

Not on its own. A pet with a 15 second grow time and a small harvest still earns less than a slow pet with a huge one. Compare pets with similar income.

Why do some pets have no grow time?

Their wiki page does not list one. They are left out rather than shown with a guess.
